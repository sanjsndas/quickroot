Grey & White Cookie Stewardship

We use cookies to cultivate a better browsing experience — just like we tend to silver-leaved perennials. Learn how we nourish your privacy while you explore our monochrome garden world.

🌱 Our approach to cookies

At Quick Root Stories, we believe in transparency that mirrors the clean simplicity of grey and white gardens. Cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our plant guides, design journals, and the 78k+ words of horticultural wisdom. This policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them.

✨ Rooted promise: We never sell your data. Any cookie information is used exclusively to improve our content, remember your preferences (like garden zone or language), and deliver a seamless grey-white gardening experience.

🍪 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They act like little seeds that store useful information — from remembering your favorite silver foliage articles to helping us analyze which stories resonate with our community. Cookies are not harmful and do not contain viruses.

🤝 Third-party cookies & gardening partners

We occasionally embed content from trusted partners such as YouTube (for our virtual garden tours) and Pinterest (for mood boards). These platforms may set their own cookies according to their policies. We only use these to enrich your experience with videos about white garden designs or grey foliage inspiration. You can block these via browser settings.

⚙️ How to manage or disable cookies

You are always in control. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. Below are quick links to adjust settings for the most popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome settings — Manage cookies from 'Privacy and Security' section.
  • Safari (macOS/iOS) — Prevent cross-site tracking & block all cookies.
  • Firefox — Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge — Site permissions.

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect core functionality, such as submitting contact forms or accessing the grey-white plant database. But your reading experience will remain mostly intact.

❓ Frequently asked: Cookies edition

Do cookies help me remember my favorite silver plants?
Yes! Preference cookies store your favorite plant lists and recently viewed grey-foliage profiles so you can pick up where you left off.
Will you use cookies to show me ads?
No intrusive advertising. We never sell data for ad targeting. Any analytics are strictly to improve our free gardening journal.
Can I still browse without analytics cookies?
Absolutely. Essential cookies remain active for basic site functions, but you can disable analytics and optional cookies via the manage button above or browser settings.
