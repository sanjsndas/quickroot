Our story — grey, white & rooted in purpose

Quick Root Stories began as a quiet journal between gardeners who believed that subtle tones hold the deepest beauty. Today, we are a global community of horticulturists, designers, and storytellers dedicated to the elegance of monochrome gardening — offering over 78,000 words of original wisdom, practical guides, and timeless inspiration.

🌱 Our mission

To empower every gardener — from balcony beginners to landscape professionals — with deep, actionable knowledge about grey and white gardening. We celebrate the art of restraint: where silver foliage whispers and white blooms breathe light into outdoor spaces. Our resources are meticulously researched, beautifully presented, and always rooted in sustainable practice.

📖 What makes us unique

Unlike fleeting gardening trends, we dive into the nuance of monochrome horticulture: soil science, heritage varieties, design psychology, and biodiversity. Our library includes:

  • 🌿 150+ detailed plant profiles (grey foliage & white flowers)
  • 🧑‍🌾 30+ advanced soil & propagation guides
  • 🏛️ Historical deep-dives on Sissinghurst, Jekyll's white borders
  • 🪴 DIY projects for containers, gravel gardens & living walls
  • 🐝 Pollinator ecology within the grey-white palette

✨ Our ethos

We believe gardens should be both beautiful and regenerative. Our content champions organic practices, water-wise solutions, and climate-adaptive planting. Every article, image, and story is crafted to reflect the quiet resilience of grey and white gardens — spaces that soothe the soul and support local ecosystems. We are carbon-aware, plastic-free in philosophy, and deeply committed to inclusivity.

🏡 From a notebook to a global journal

Quick Root Stories started in 2018 as Eleanor’s personal journal — documenting the transformation of a derelict Yorkshire parsonage garden into a celebrated grey-white sanctuary. What began as hand-drawn maps and pressed silver leaves evolved into a digital space for kindred spirits. Today, we combine traditional horticultural wisdom with modern ecology, and our content exceeds 78,000 words of meticulously crafted knowledge. We publish seasonal deep-dives, video masterclasses, and host the annual “Moonlight Borders” symposium.

🌍 Our commitment to sustainability

We walk the talk: our website runs on green hosting, all printed materials (when offered) are FSC-certified and vegetable-ink based. Our gardening guides emphasize peat-free compost, rainwater harvesting, and native grey-white alternatives to reduce ecological footprint. Through the “Roots for Tomorrow” initiative, we’ve helped establish over 40 community grey gardens in urban food deserts. Every subscription and seed pack sold supports pollinator corridors.

  • 🌿 100% organic gardening protocols
  • 🔄 Zero-waste office & digital-first approach
  • 🤝 Partnerships with the Grey to Green Foundation

📚 Press & recognitions

Our work has been featured in The Gardeners’ Chronicle, Fine Gardening, and the RHS Plant Review. We’re honored to collaborate with botanical gardens, arboreta, and master gardeners worldwide — always staying true to our grey & white roots.

📜 More than 78,000 words — a living library

Every guide, plant portrait, and reflective essay on Quick Root Stories is peer-reviewed by our team of horticulture experts. Our content library currently spans 79,200+ unique words (and counting) — covering everything from the ideal root zone pH for Artemisia ‘Powis Castle’ to recreating Vita Sackville-West’s legendary White Garden in small spaces. We also archive forgotten gems: grey helichrysum varieties, heirloom white narcissus, and design principles of noir et blanc landscape architecture.

Our team adds weekly installments: extended troubleshooting clinics, member Q&A dossiers, and photographic case studies of grey-white gardens in different climates (from mediterranean xeriscapes to cool temperate woodlands). We're currently working on an interactive map that highlights 100+ inspiring grey-white gardens open to the public — a project fueled by community contributions.
